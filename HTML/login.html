The basics

What is a loan?

A loan is money a lender provides upfront, which you repay over time in fixed installments, plus interest. It's a tool — used well, it lets you act on a need today and settle the cost gradually, on agreed terms.

Personal Loan

Unsecured funds for any personal need — medical costs, travel, or consolidating other debt — repaid over a fixed term.

Home Loan

Long-tenure financing secured against the property itself, built to keep monthly payments proportionate to income.

Business Loan

Working capital or expansion funding sized to your business's cash flow, with repayment schedules that flex around it.

Before you apply

Eligibility

A quick check against these conditions before any paperwork begins.

  • Age 21–58

    At the time of application, and within the lending limit at the end of the loan term.

  • Steady, verifiable income

    Salaried or self-employed, with at least 12 months of continuous income history.

  • Valid identity & address proof

    Government-issued ID and a recent address document in your name.

  • No active default on record

    A clean standing with no unresolved default on a prior loan or credit line.
